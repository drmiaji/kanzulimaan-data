Little Ummah Quiz Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 September 2026

Little Ummah Quiz is a children’s Islamic learning app designed for children and families. The app works primarily offline and is designed to minimize the collection and sharing of personal information.

Information stored on the device

The app may store the following information locally on the user’s device:

  • A child’s first name or nickname entered by a parent or guardian
  • Optional age, grade or class, and selected avatar
  • Quiz answers, scores, progress, achievements and completed quizzes
  • Questions saved in My Review List
  • Language, theme, accessibility and other app settings
  • A selected city and its coordinates for Prayer Times and Qibla
  • Prayer reminder and notification preferences

This information is used only to provide the app’s learning, personalization, progress, Prayer Times and Qibla features. It is not transmitted to the developer.

Information we do not collect

The developer does not collect, receive or sell personal information through the app. The app does not include:

  • Advertising or advertising identifiers
  • Analytics or behavioral tracking
  • Account registration or cloud accounts
  • Social networking or public profiles
  • In-app purchases

The app does not request access to precise or approximate device location, contacts, camera, microphone, phone information or media files.

Prayer Times and Qibla

A parent or guardian can select or enter a city. The app first checks a bundled offline city catalog. If the entered city is not available in that catalog, Android’s system geocoding service may process the city text according to the device provider’s own privacy terms. The resulting coordinates are stored locally and used by both Prayer Times and Qibla.

Prayer times are calculated estimates and may vary from local mosque timetables or religious authorities.

Notifications and prayer reminders

If enabled by an adult, the app uses Android notification, alarm, foreground-service, wake-lock and restart capabilities to provide prayer reminders and play an adhan. Notification and reminder preferences remain on the device.

Sharing

The app opens Android’s system share sheet only when a user deliberately chooses to share a quiz result or certificate. The user chooses the receiving application. Any information sent to that application is then handled under the receiving application’s privacy policy.

Data retention and deletion

Information remains on the device until it is changed or deleted by the user. A parent can delete an individual child profile from Parent Settings. This removes that profile’s results, progress and review list from the app’s local database.

Uninstalling the app normally removes its local data. Android backup or device-transfer services may retain or restore app data according to the user’s device settings and the service provider’s privacy policy.

Children’s privacy

Little Ummah Quiz is designed for children and families. A parent or guardian should create and manage child profiles. Only a first name or nickname is needed; age and grade are optional. The app does not knowingly transmit children’s personal information to the developer or use it for advertising, profiling or marketing.

Security

App information is stored within the app’s private storage on the device. No method of electronic storage is completely secure, but the app minimizes risk by avoiding developer-operated accounts, analytics and remote storage.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app’s features or legal requirements change. The current version will be published at this location with a revised “Last updated” date.
